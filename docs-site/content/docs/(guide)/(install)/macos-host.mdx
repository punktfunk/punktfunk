---
title: "macOS Host"
description: "Run the Punktfunk host on a Mac: a menu bar app with its own virtual display, controllers and web console."
---

Stream a Mac's desktop or games to any Punktfunk client, at the client's resolution and refresh
rate, HDR included, with no second monitor. To stream *to* a Mac, see the
[Apple client](/docs/clients#apple-app-mac-iphone-ipad-apple-tv-vision-pro).

The host is one app, **Punktfunk Host**, that lives in the menu bar. It runs as you, in your
login session, so it streams while you are logged in. Keep it on a trusted network and read
[Security & Safe Use](/docs/security).

## What you need

- **A Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 15 or newer.**
- Nothing else. Capture, the H.264 and HEVC encoders, audio and the virtual display are part of
  macOS; the app carries its web console and plugin runner.

## Install

1. Download `Punktfunk-Host-<ver>.dmg` from the
   [latest release](https://git.unom.io/unom/punktfunk/releases) and open it.
2. Drag **Punktfunk Host** onto **Applications**.
3. Open it from Applications. Its icon appears in the menu bar and a setup window opens.

The setup window lists the permissions on one page, then asks about starting at login. Each
**Allow** button shows macOS's own prompt the first time; after that it opens the matching page
of System Settings.

| Step | What it allows | In System Settings → Privacy & Security |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Screen recording** | Sending the screen to the device you stream to | Screen & System Audio Recording |
| **Keyboard and mouse** | Typing and moving the pointer from that device | Accessibility (macOS 27: Device Control and Data Access) |
| **System audio** | Sending the Mac's sound | Screen & System Audio Recording → System Audio Recording Only |
| **Start at login** | Starting the host whenever you log in | General → Login Items & Extensions |

macOS also asks once to let Punktfunk Host find devices on your local network. Allow it, or
clients can't find or reach the host.

After you allow screen recording or keyboard and mouse, the page offers **Reopen Punktfunk
Host**: macOS applies those two only to a freshly started app. The setup window comes back
where you left it.

The last page shows the web console's password. Copy it: the console asks for it when you sign
in from another device.

Controllers need no permission: the app is signed to present them itself.

## Pair

Choose **Pair a Device** in the menu bar item and type the PIN it shows on the device. A device
that asked to connect on its own appears there too, with **Approve** and **Deny**.

**Next:** [Quick Start, step 3](/docs/quickstart#3-open-the-web-console) for the same in the web
console.

## Good to know

- **Menu bar item.** It shows whether the host is idle or streaming, who is connected and at
  what mode, and has **End Session**, **Pair a Device**, **Open Web Console**, **Start at
  Login**, **Open Logs Folder**, **Restart Punktfunk Host** and **Quit Punktfunk Host**. Quit
  stops the host; with **Start at Login** on it comes back at your next login.
- **Web console.** **Open Web Console** signs you in. From another device it is
  `https://<this Mac's address>:47992`; the setup window's last page shows the first password,
  and `~/.config/punktfunk/web-password` holds it until you sign in with it once.
- **Settings.** Change them in the console under **Host**. A line in
  `~/.config/punktfunk/host.env` pins a setting; **Restart Punktfunk Host** applies the file.
  See [Configuration](/docs/configuration).
- **Displays.** Each session gets its own display at the client's mode, in HDR when the client
  asks. The host can stream a real monitor instead: see
  [Stream a real monitor](/docs/stream-a-monitor).
- **Controllers.** A client's controller reaches games as a DualSense, DualShock 4, Switch Pro
  Controller or Xbox Wireless Controller, with rumble, motion, touchpad and light bar where the
  real pad has them.
- **Pen and touch.** A stylus arrives as a drawing tablet, with pressure, tilt and the eraser.
  macOS has no touch screens, so touch works as it does with Sidecar: a tap clicks, a pan
  scrolls, a long press right-clicks, and two fingers zoom and rotate.
- **Monthly prompt.** macOS asks about once a month whether Punktfunk Host may keep recording
  the screen. Answer it at the Mac, or the next session shows nothing.
- **Plugins.** The plugin runner is part of the app, and each plugin runs in its own sandbox.
  Install plugins from the console's store: [Plugin setup](/docs/plugin-setup).
- **Logs.** `~/.config/punktfunk/logs/` holds `host.log`, `web.log` and `runner.log`.
  `RUST_LOG=debug` in `host.env` adds detail.
- **Updates.** The console says when a new version is out and links to its disk image. Quit
  the host, drag the new app over the old one and open it. Your permissions stay.
- **Not on a Mac host:** the microphone, streaming before you log in, and the restart and
  shut-down power actions (sleep works).

## Uninstall

Turn **Start at Login** off, choose **Quit Punktfunk Host** and move the app to the Trash.
`~/.config/punktfunk` holds the host's identity, pairings and settings; delete it to forget
them. Remove the app's entries under **Privacy & Security** in System Settings if you want
them gone.
