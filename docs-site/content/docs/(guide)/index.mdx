**Punktfunk** streams your desktop and games from a Linux or Windows PC to your other devices at
low latency. Each device gets a virtual display at its own resolution and refresh rate, encoded on
the GPU. Native apps cover Mac, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Windows, Linux and Android, and any
Moonlight client works too.
